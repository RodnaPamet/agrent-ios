## Description (4000)
Agrent is a farm-management app for farmers in Bulgaria. It keeps the farm's records in one place and takes them to the field. The app is in Bulgarian.

CROP-PROTECTION AND FERTILISER DIARY
• Record a spraying or fertilising from the field: product, dose, parcels, date.
• The app produces the diary as a PDF in the format the Bulgarian Food Safety Agency (БАБХ) requires.
• Agrent, the built-in assistant, also fills the record from a sentence: "I sprayed parcel 12 with …".

MAP OF YOUR BLOCKS
• Every block and parcel on the map, with area and crop.
• The Ministry of Agriculture's orthophoto map and physical blocks.
• Satellite vegetation index and water-stress risk per parcel.
• Your own name for each parcel — the diary keeps its number.

DASHBOARD
• Weather now and for the week, rainfall for the last 30 days and the forecast.
• Three months of prices for your crops — national average purchase prices from МЗХ / САПИ.
• Harvest and sowing progress across the country.

TASKS FOR THE TEAM
• Tasks for machine operators with their parcels on the map and a tick per parcel.
• An operator sees only their own tasks.

AGRONOMIC CALENDAR
• Sowing, field-work and harvest windows per crop, for North and South Bulgaria, with the source.
• Economic pest thresholds from the Ministry's guides.
• Coupled-support rates per decare.

EXCHANGE AND MESSAGES
• Grain and oilseed listings, searchable by region.
• Messages with buyers and sellers; channels by crop and region.

COSTS AND CALCULATOR
• Costs per crop and overheads, margin per decare.

Each farm sees only its own records.
