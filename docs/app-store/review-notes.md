# App Review information

Paste into App Store Connect → the version → App Review Information.

## Sign-in information

The app signs in only with **Sign in with Apple**, **Google** or **Microsoft**. There is no email-and-password form in the iPhone app.

Fill in ONE of these before submitting (owner's step):
- **Recommended:** a dedicated **Google** account for review, with no 2-step verification, that is a member of a demo farm with sample data. Put its email and password in "User name" and "Password".
- **Or:** tick "Sign-in required" and say that the reviewer may use **Sign in with Apple** with their own Apple ID. The app then creates an empty account and walks them through creating a farm (the «Ново стопанство» wizard). Every screen works, but with no data.

## Notes (English, paste as is)

Agrent is a farm-management app for farmers in Bulgaria. Its interface is in Bulgarian only.

HOW TO SIGN IN
Use the Google account above (or Sign in with Apple, see Sign-in information). The demo farm has blocks, parcels, crop-protection records, tasks and costs.

WHAT TO LOOK AT
• Табло (Dashboard): weather where you are, rainfall for 30 days, prices of the farm's crops.
• Карта (Map): the farm's blocks and parcels; the map-mode button cycles to the Ministry of Agriculture's orthophoto map.
• Дневник (Journal): crop-protection records; «Нов запис» records a treatment and produces the legally required diary as a PDF.
• Задачи (Tasks), Борса (Exchange), Календари (Calendars) and Агрент (the assistant, the round button at the bottom right).

LOCATION
The app asks for location "While Using". It is used to (1) show the user's position on the farm map and the parcel they are standing in, which is never sent anywhere, and (2) show the weather and rainfall on the Dashboard. For (2) the position is rounded to about 5 km (at most two decimal places) before it is sent to Apple Weather (WeatherKit) and Open-Meteo. Without permission, the weather is for the centre of the farm's blocks.

ACCOUNT DELETION
Menu (top left) → «Админ» → the account row at the top (Профил) → «Изтриване на профила» → «Изтрий профила». The account is frozen at once and deleted after a 30-day grace period, during which the user can restore it.

PAYMENTS
None. The app has no in-app purchases and no paid features, and shows no links to pay outside the app.

AI ASSISTANT
«Агрент» answers questions about the farm's own records and can prepare a journal entry. The user reviews every prepared entry in the normal form and saves it themselves; the assistant never writes on its own.

THIRD-PARTY DATA
Weather: Apple Weather (attribution shown). Rainfall: Open-Meteo. Prices: Bulgarian Ministry of Agriculture (МЗХ / САПИ) and the European Commission. Orthophoto map: МЗХ's public INSPIRE service.
